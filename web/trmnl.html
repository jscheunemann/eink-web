Why the menus are sideways

CrossPoint draws its menus in portrait only, so on a landscape TRMNL they appear on their side. That is what the unmodified firmware would show on real hardware, and the main thing a port would change. Books open in landscape: this SD card starts with Reading Orientation set to Landscape CCW. The Resume, Select, Up and Down tabs along the right edge are hints for the stand-in board's four front buttons; a TRMNL port would drop them.
